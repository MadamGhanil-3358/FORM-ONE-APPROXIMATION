Meaning of Approximation | Maana ya Ukadiriaji

📌 Approximation is a process of finding a number that is closest to the exact value.
It involves expressing a number into a higher or lower value which is close to the exact value. Approximation can be termed as estimation and is represented by the symbol '≈' (approximately equal to).
📌 Ukadiriaji ni mchakato wa kutafuta nambari iliyo karibu zaidi na thamani halisi.
Inahusisha kueleza nambari kuwa thamani ya juu au ya chini iliyo karibu na thamani halisi. Ukadiriaji unaweza kuitwa makadirio na unawakilishwa na alama '≈' (takriban sawa).
⭐ "≈" means approximately equal to | inamaanisha takriban sawa na

Rounding off by Place Values | Kuzungusha kwa Thamani ya Mahali

🎯 Rounding to the nearest ten, hundred, thousand, etc.
Example: 473 rounded to nearest hundred → 500 (because 7 tens ≥ 50).
Rule: Look at the digit to the right. If it's 5 or more → round up. If less than 5 → round down.
🎯 Kuzungusha kwa kumi, mia, elfu iliyo karibu zaidi.
Mfano: 473 kwa mia iliyo karibu → 500 (kwa sababu 7 makumi ≥ 50).
Kanuni: Angalia tarakimu ya kulia. Ikiwa ni 5 au zaidi → zungusha juu. Ikiwa chini ya 5 → zungusha chini.
📐 Examples: 47→50 (nearest ten) | 132→100 (nearest hundred)
🔢🧮

Rounding by Decimal Places | Kuzungusha kwa Sehemu za Desimali

🎯 Rounding to 1 decimal place, 2 decimal places, etc.
Example: 3.768 rounded to 2 decimal places → 3.77 (third digit is 8 ≥ 5, so round up).
Keep the required digits, look at the next digit.
🎯 Kuzungusha hadi desimali moja, mbili, nk.
Mfano: 3.768 hadi desimali mbili → 3.77 (tarakimu ya tatu ni 8 ≥ 5, hivyo zungusha juu).
Weka tarakimu unazotaka, angalia ile inayofuata.
🔍 7.2849 → 7.3 (1 d.p) | 12.345 → 12.35 (2 d.p)
